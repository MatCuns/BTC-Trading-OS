# BTC Quant OS — visual identity

## Product description
Local-first BTC quantitative trading platform for Windows: OKX market data, paper execution, local AI intelligence, independent risk controls and a research terminal. Under development.

## Visual system
Midnight navy background (#08111F), turquoise intelligence accents (#45E0C1), warm Bitcoin amber (#FFBA64), slate grid and off-white typography. The design favors data density, clear environment labels and readable negative space.

Assets are editable, repository-native SVGs in docs/assets. Hero is product branding. Terminal is explicitly a concept with illustrative curves and no performance claims. Do not present it as an application screenshot.
